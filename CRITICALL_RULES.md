# CritiCall Practice Rules

## Exercise rotation

- Maximum **4 consecutive items** of any one exercise type.
- Rotate after 4 at the latest.
- Keep the overall sequence deliberately randomized/freestyle.

## Scoring discipline

- Do not substitute a real-world rule when a CritiCall-specific rule has been established.
- If uncertain about a test-specific answer, say so rather than confidently teaching a conflicting rule.
- Preserve benchmark results exactly as reported.

## Typing

- Standard comparison test: 5 minutes, English 1k, Blind Mode ON, caret OFF.
- CritiCall transcription target: **≥40 WPM and ≥95% accuracy**.
- Protect accuracy while maintaining passing speed.
- The custom simulator ignores trailing whitespace at line/paragraph ends.

## Audio drills

- Use actual audio for exercises designated as audio.
- Use short natural chunks and small pauses.
- Numeric memory: maximum 7 digits.
- Do not expose the answer visually before an audio spelling or memory attempt.

## Data entry

- ALL CAPS.
- Repeat every name 5 times after initial entry.
- Reinforce difficult names/streets as needed.
- Do not penalize an omitted licence-plate space when all characters are correct.

## Spelling reinforcement

- Prefer audio presentation.
- Show the correct spelling after the attempt and use 5 repetitions for reinforcement.
- Recycle recurring misses in later randomized sessions.

## Decision Making classification key

### Police
Attempted/threatened physical harm, actual physical harm, or harm to another person's property.

### Fire
Immediate signs of fire such as flames or smoke, a fire alarm, or a trapped/confined person needing rescue or release.

### EMS
An emergency medical condition requiring intervention by medically trained personnel.

### Public Utility
Malfunctioning or broken public water systems, electrical power systems (including power lines, streetlights and traffic signals), natural-gas systems used for home heating, or blocked sewer drainpipes.

**Training-specific reminder: natural gas → Utility.**

## Call summarization

Capture the essentials without adding assumptions:

1. Location
2. Service/agency need
3. What happened
4. Immediate danger
5. Injuries/medical condition
6. Weapons
7. Suspect/person/vehicle description and direction
8. Updates

Report observations separately from caller conclusions or guesses.
